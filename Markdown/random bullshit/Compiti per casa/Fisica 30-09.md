$\Huge{\mathbf{Esercizio\ }4.}$


$$
B= \frac{F}{|q|v\sin \theta}= \frac{5.4 \times 10^{-3} \text{ N}}{|-8.3 \times 10^{-6} \text{ C }| \cdot 7.4 \times 10^{6} \text{ m/s } \cdot \sin(52\degree)} \approx 1.1 \times 10^{-5} \text{ T}
$$

$\Huge{\mathbf{Esercizio\ } 5.}$


$$
B= \frac{F}{qv\sin \theta} \implies v= \frac{F}{qB\sin \theta}= \frac{5.9 \times 10^{-20} \text{ N}}{1.6 \times 10^{-19} \text{ C } \cdot 3.4 \times 10^{-5} \text{ T}} \approx 1.1 \times 10^{4} \text{ m/s }
$$

$\Huge{\mathbf{Esercizio\ } 6.}$

 
 $$
 B = \frac{F}{qv\sin \theta} \implies \sin \theta = \frac{F}{qvB}= \frac{4.6 \times 10^{-7} \text{ N}}{5.3 \times 10^{-9} \text{ C } \cdot 4.9 \times 10^{4} \text{ m/s } \cdot 3.1 \times 10^{-3} \text{ T}} \approx 0.57
 $$

Applichiamo la funzione inversa e ricaviamo l'angolo acuto:


$$
\theta = \arcsin(\sin \theta)=\arcsin(0.57) \approx 35\degree
$$
