Novels were sold in bookshops or circulating libraries,  the less wealthy could buy novels published in instalments in newspapers.

Writers and readers both came from the middle class, so they could relate to each other, and the writer could have a public of readers. 

According to the established order, people could not ascend the social ladder, but during the victorian age, with the affirmation of the middle class, people could become rich by investing.

The father managed the family, there was no divorce; when a girl got married, she lost her property. Women wrote with a pseudonym, for example Charlotte Bronte and Emily Bronte.

In 1851 Prince Albert organized the Great Exhibition, showcasing the great  scientific and technological advances at the Crystal Palace (a palace in glass and steel, because these materials were easier to find and costed less).
