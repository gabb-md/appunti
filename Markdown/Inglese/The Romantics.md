# The birth of Romanticism

The **Industrial Revolution** brought about immense changes to society and the way the world conceived work, it was a time of **dramatic changes** in every aspect of life, down to politics, economics, science, labor and, the subject of our study, arts and literature. 

On the morning of **21st January 1793**, in the square of Paris, was erected a contraption simply known as ***The Machine***, proposed by ***Joseph Guillottin***.  At about 10 o'clock that morning, a man lowered his head to the level of a wooden support. The executioner released the blade, which lodged itself into the man's neck. The blade was released a second time; this time, the man's head detatched from his body and fell into a basket. That man was **Louis XVI** the king of France.

It all began **40 years before**, in a world controlled by the elements of hierarchy and authority, there were **no options of climbing the social ladder**. Just the nobles had dignity, money, liberty, and the clergy that had a decent amount of freedom. The other people had no rights, only duties.

In Paris, **police officers were ubiqutous**, about 1 in 545 men worked in the police force. The *"undesired"* people would just disappear. In 1742 two men sit at a café to play chess. They shared their ideas, that became the seeds of Romanticism many years later. These men were ***Denis Diderot* and *Jean-Jacques Rosseau***, they had different ideas but were united by their disliking of the system. Diderot thought the future would be founded on reason, whereas Rousseau belieed in the power of feelings.
